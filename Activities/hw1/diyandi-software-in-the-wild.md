## 1. User group

**Who are you designing for?**  
Event Attendees and EVent Holders

**Why might this group need support during Diyandi?**  
A Fiesta is an important and recognizable part of the filipino culture and the Diyandi Festival is a prime example of it. However besides the festivities, games, events and whatever things might happen, above all this is the fact that Food is one of the True Main focus and what is always a constant in fiesta's is food, as mention by Edge Genosa in his article "The Filipino Fiesta", "When one mentions the words “Filipino fiesta”, the first picture that should come to mind is food. A LOT of food." and together with the Filipino's innate culture of bayanihan its part of the feista and somewhat even expected that some houses are open to go to even for random people that are just visiting. The problem is this open invitation can only travel through word of mouth so a tourist that has only come to Iligan for the first time has no idea where to go. Event Holders meanwhile are feeding people as a form of tradition and panata to their patron, an example presented by an article saying "The fact remains that people will always invite you to eat at their place, even when you tell them you're just passing by, or that you just wanted to see the parade, and then go your way after." As such they will want visitors to share the food and the spirit of the event.

A tool similar Grab/Uber apps that would utilize a map that would be marked by which houses/locations are avialable to be visted. ## 5. Things users can do

Describe **two specific actions** that users could perform using your proposed system.

1. The Atendee can search up locations in which available for a visit and then view information regarding the place they will visit and the specifics noted by the event owner. They can mark an event location as unsafe. 

2. The holder can list their location as available for a visit, specify how many can visit and how many they can accomodate. And take down the listing if they are no longer available. They can also mark an antendee as rude

Naturally you'd need a verified account with a verified ID to be able to list and visit, theres no need for a seperate account for a holder or atendee a single account can do both even at the same time.
